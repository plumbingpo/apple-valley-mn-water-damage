How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now

When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Apple Valley

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor

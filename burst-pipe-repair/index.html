Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

Apple Valley Restoration Co provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Apple Valley and surrounding areas. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Open sink cabinets and leave a slow drip running on cold nights, which helps keep pipes from freezing
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.
